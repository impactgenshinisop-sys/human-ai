# Human AI Engine (`/src/human_ai/`)

This folder contains **100% pure Human Intelligence, Learning, Skills, Genetics, Utility/GOAP Decision-Making, Episodic Memory, Social Relationships, and Collective Research logic**.

It contains **zero graphics**, **zero visual appearance/clothing/sprites**, and **zero terrain/biome/world generation**.

---

## Folder Structure

1. **`types.ts`** — All pure cognitive data structures (`HumanBrainState`, `PersonalityVector`, `GeneticAptitudes`, `HumanSkillSet`, `VitalNeeds`, `CognitiveGoalType`, `GOAPDecisionResult`, `EpisodicMemory`, `SocialBond`).
2. **`personalityAndGenetics.ts`** — 5-axis continuous personality generation (`diligence`, `curiosity`, `sociability`, `courage`, `piety`), cognitive aptitudes (`intelligence`, `strength`, `fertility`), genetic crossover + mutation from parents to children, and 5 developmental life stages (`Baby` → `Child` → `Teen` → `Adult` → `Elder`).
3. **`skillsAndLearning.ts`** — Experiential "learning by doing" across 7 skills (`farming`, `mining`, `smithing`, `carpentry`, `combat`, `scholarship`, `seafaring`), asymptotic diminishing returns, intelligence & curiosity learning rate multipliers, Mentor → Apprentice knowledge transfer, mastery ranks (`Novice` to `Grandmaster`), and dynamic career adaptation via comparative advantage.
4. **`needsAndUtilityGOAP.ts`** — Maslow biological & psychological drives (`satiety`, `warmthAndRest`, `safety`, `socialBelonging`), circadian daily schedule slots (`Dawn`, `MorningShift`, `MiddayMeal`, `AfternoonShift`, `EveningSocial`, `NightRest`), moral compliance vs. desperation theft under starvation, non-linear Utility AI goal scoring, and multi-step GOAP action plans with internal monologues.
5. **`memoryAndSocialBonds.ts`** — Autobiographical episodic memory buffer with importance-based memory consolidation, cosine-like personality compatibility, friendship/rivalry affinity dynamics, marriage formation, mentorship pairing, and Hall-of-Fame Notable promotion.
6. **`collectiveIntelligence.ts`** — How individual human learning, scholarship, and empirical practice aggregate into collective civilization `knowledgePoints`, unlocking technologies and advancing across 6 historical eras (`Stone Age` → `Arcane Renaissance`).
7. **`HumanAIEngine.ts`** — Self-contained orchestrator class (`HumanAIEngine`) that steps human brains through perception, needs, GOAP decision-making, experiential skill learning, social bonding, and yearly aging.
